# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone who works on, reviews, or is given
authorized access to Nexus — the Veloxs AI Inc. team and any approved
collaborators. Nexus is proprietary software; this policy governs
professional conduct, not participation in a public community.

## Expected behavior

- Treat colleagues and collaborators with respect, empathy, and
  professionalism.
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Respect the confidentiality of Nexus source code, data, and any
  customer or pilot information you encounter.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Unauthorized disclosure of proprietary or confidential information.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to **conduct@veloxs.ai**. Reports are reviewed promptly and
fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is respected. Veloxs AI Inc. may
take any action it deems appropriate, up to and including revoking repository
access and ending the working or collaboration relationship.

Questions about this policy can also be directed to conduct@veloxs.ai.
